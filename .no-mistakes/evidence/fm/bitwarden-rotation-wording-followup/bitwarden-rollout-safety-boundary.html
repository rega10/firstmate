What this runbook never authorizes

Migration-batch approval or completion never authorizes deleting, invalidating, rotating, or retiring existing custody or credentials as part of the migration, including old-custody retirement; the independently triggered joiner/mover/leaver rotation procedure and rotation-after-exposure procedure remain in force.
Creating accounts or organizations, choosing or purchasing a plan, inviting users, changing billing, reading or moving secret values, or changing production access require the captain's direct participation or explicit approval at the gate that names them.
